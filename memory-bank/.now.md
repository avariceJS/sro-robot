## Critical unfinished risks

1. No durable `write_result_unknown` state or write-attempt record, no cross-task identity reservation, no atomic task claim, no SRO read-back verification.
2. Runner may treat dry-run close as generic `done`; Save success is not proven by reopening the record.
3. MKAD selects the first visible published/PDF candidate, has a fixed 1.2-second wait and does not validate the downloaded PDF's case number.
4. Extracted FIO/INN/address are not proven to belong to the debtor participant; act type/operation selection remains unsafe for ambiguous documents.
5. Upload hashes identical files but does not prevent duplicate tasks; large batch progress/recovery is immature.
6. Shared SRO account/profile and one SRO worker limit throughput; review holds the worker up to 10 minutes. No load or concurrency evidence for 20+ staff.
7. No demonstrated production migrations, backup/restore, monitoring/metrics, security/RBAC review, deployment runbook or recovery drill. `create_all` is not a migration system.
